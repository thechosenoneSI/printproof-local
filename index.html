LOCAL BY DESIGN
Your artwork isn’t the upload.
The app downloads open-source OCR code and English model weights from a public CDN. Your image and approved copy are processed here, in memory. No accounts, analytics, or inference server.
EVIDENCE, NOT GUESSWORK
See the actual pixels.
Every located difference comes with an artwork crop and OCR confidence. “Not found” means the model didn’t read it. You make the final call.
KNOW THE LIMITS
Words, not a full preflight.
English PNG/JPEG artwork only. This checks approved text, not bleed, color, fonts, layout, extra unapproved copy, or print quality. Stylized and tiny text can fail.


NO PERFECT-ACCURACY CLAIMS
Test it. See where it breaks.
Run the same neural OCR on synthetic controls and deliberately flawed artwork. These are small reproducible examples, not customer results.
